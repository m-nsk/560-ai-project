\begin{enumerate}
\item What is the main goal of approximation algorithms?
(A) To find the optimal solution
(B) To find a solution close to optimal
(C) To reduce the running time
(D) To increase the memory usage

\item What is the approximation ratio of an algorithm?
(A) The smallest $\alpha$ such that $ALG \leq \alpha \cdot OPT$
(B) The largest $\alpha$ such that $ALG \geq \alpha \cdot OPT$
(C) The average load of the machines
(D) The maximum load of the machines

\item What is the goal of the vertex cover problem?
(A) To find a minimum subset of vertices that covers all edges
(B) To find a maximum subset of vertices that covers all edges
(C) To find a minimum subset of edges that covers all vertices
(D) To find a maximum subset of edges that covers all vertices

\item What is the algorithm for vertex cover?
(A) Add one endpoint of each edge to $S$
(B) Add both endpoints of each edge to $S$
(C) Remove one endpoint of each edge from $G$
(D) Remove both endpoints of each edge from $G$

\item What is the approximation ratio of the vertex cover algorithm?
(A) 1
(B) 2
(C) 3
(D) 4

\item What is the goal of the load balancing problem?
(A) To minimize the average load
(B) To minimize the maximum load
(C) To maximize the average load
(D) To maximize the maximum load

\item What is the algorithm for load balancing?
(A) Assign each job to the machine with the largest load
(B) Assign each job to the machine with the smallest load
(C) Assign each job to a random machine
(D) Assign each job to the machine with the most jobs

\item What is the approximation ratio of the load balancing algorithm?
(A) 1
(B) 2
(C) 3
(D) $2 - \frac{1}{m}$

\item What is the makespan of an assignment?
(A) The minimum load created by that assignment
(B) The maximum load created by that assignment
(C) The average load created by that assignment
(D) The total load created by that assignment

\item What is the optimal makespan?
(A) The minimum makespan over all assignments
(B) The maximum makespan over all assignments
(C) The average makespan over all assignments
(D) The total makespan over all assignments

\item What is the relationship between $T$ and $T^*$?
(A) $T \leq T^*$
(B) $T \geq T^*$
(C) $T \leq 2T^*$
(D) $T \geq 2T^*$

\end{enumerate}
